# INK & PAPER · 水墨书斋

A look for Claude Code in the spirit of a scholar's study: rice paper by day, ink under a lamp by night.

## What it changes

- **Colors**, in both modes. Light mode is warm rice paper (宣纸) with dark ink text. Dark mode is lamp-lit ink, a version of its own rather than an inversion of the light one. Links and keywords are mineral blue (石青), and red, green and amber keep their meaning.
- **Backdrop** (desktop app): an ink-wash landscape of three ranges of mountains, two bands of mist drifting very slowly, a vermilion sun (a pale moon in dark mode) and a small boat. Under reduced motion the mist holds still.
- **Messages**: set in a serif face. Your messages are paper boxes with a vermilion seal (印章) stamped at the top-right corner. Claude's replies are quiet paper slips.
- **Status line**: the current solar term (节气) and double hour (时辰), for example `🖌 小雪 · 未时`. The solar terms use their usual Gregorian dates, so they can be a day early or late in some years.
- **A small surprise**: a passing test run stamps a 「准」 seal.

## Commands

- `/ink` or `/ink on` turns the look on. It starts off after you install it, and stays the way you leave it across sessions.
- `/ink off` turns it off. The choice is kept across sessions.

Only one look shows at a time. When you turn this one on, its reply names any other look mod from this collection that is still on (`neon-pilot`, `terminal-1983`, `ink-paper`, `seasons`) and the command that turns it off.

The terminal draws no look, but shows the status line and the 「准」 toast.
